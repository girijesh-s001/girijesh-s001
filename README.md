## About

I am a B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Bannari Amman Institute of Technology (2024–2028, CGPA 7.8). My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, OCR, and document intelligence — with a particular focus on low-resource language technology and systems that communicate not just *what* they predicted, but *why*.

> **Engineering philosophy:** Prediction alone is not enough. Every output should carry a confidence score, a localized heatmap, and a written rationale — so the system can be understood, trusted, and acted on.

**Current research interests:** Low-resource OCR · Ancient Tamil script recognition · Document forensics · Explainable AI · Tamil language technology

## Research

### Ancient Tamil Character Recognition
*Research paper in progress*

Built an end-to-end OCR pipeline for handwritten and palm-leaf manuscript Tamil characters, covering adaptive image preprocessing, curved text-line segmentation, and a lightweight CNN-based recognition model.

- **10 manuscripts** · **~2,200 labelled glyphs** · **319 glyph classes**
- Achieved **88.10% character accuracy** (11.90% CER)
- Benchmarked against PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5, DeepSeek-OCR, Pixtral-12B, Donut, and PARSeq
- Co-built **Olaisuvadi** — an open-source annotation platform supporting 319 Tamil glyph classes

An NLP pipeline that converts Romanized Tamil (Tanglish) into native Tamil script using text normalization and transliteration — addressing how Tamil speakers actually type in digital environments.

*Example: `"vanakkam eppadi irukeenga"` → `"வணக்கம் எப்படி இருக்கீங்க"`*
